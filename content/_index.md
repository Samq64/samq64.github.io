Hi, I'm Samuel Q! I am a volunteer maintainer for [Scratch Addons](https://scratchaddons.com), a browser extension with over half a million monthly active users. My software development skills are mostly self-taught, however I have completed [CS50x 2025](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2025/).

I have daily driven Linux on the desktop since 2022 and have gained quite a lot of command line experience because of it.

I prioritize modern, efficient and privacy respecting software with a focus on using built-in functionality over unnecessary dependencies or abstractions, yet without sacrificing much development speed or comfort.
